LinkUp Explorer

At a packed festival the mobile network is jammed, so friends who get separated cannot text each other. LinkUp lets phones pass a message hand to hand through the crowd, phone to phone, whenever two people are within 10 metres. This page replays one simulated festival so you can watch it happen.

Style
1 · THE PROBLEM

The network is jammed

Thousands of phones share one mobile tower. Bars look full, but messages do not get through.

2 · WHAT STILL WORKS

Phone to phone

Phones can still talk directly to any phone within 10 m, like Bluetooth or Wi-Fi Direct.

3 · THE IDEA

Pass it on

A message hops from person to person, carried around the festival, until someone carrying it comes near the friend.

4 · OUR QUESTION

Who should carry it?

Everyone? Nobody? Anyone you bump into? Or people who are walking? We compare four ways on the same crowd.

The message we follow

Why are the people moving?

Like real festival-goers, everyone walks between the stages, the food court, the toilets and the entrance, then stays there for a while (5–10 min at a stage). Friends move together as a group. Now and then someone wanders off alone and comes back after at least 5 minutes.

Why it matters: a message can only jump between two phones when their owners come within 10 m. Walking is what creates new meetings, and the "walker-first" method uses exactly this.

      What do these words mean?
      Priya and Arjun
      Made-up names for the sender and the friend of the message we follow, so the story is easy to tell. In the simulation they are just two numbered phones.
      Person / phone
      Each dot is one festival-goer whose phone runs LinkUp. Other attendees are not shown.
      Hand-delivery only (Direct Delivery)
      Only the sender carries the message, until they meet the friend in person.
      Copy to everyone (Epidemic)
      Every phone copies the message to every phone it meets. Fast, but floods phones with copies.
      Limited copies (Binary Spray-and-Wait, BSW)
      At most 8 copies. Each carrier gives half of its spare copies to anyone new it meets; with one copy left it just waits to meet the friend.
      Walker-first limited copies (MWS, our method)
      The same 8-copy limit, but copies go to strangers who are walking, never to the carrier's own friends; standing strangers are allowed only once the message is 60 s old.
      Walking / standing
      Filled dots are walking between areas; hollow dots are standing at a stage, the food court and so on.
      Friend group
      People who came together. They usually move together, but sometimes one wanders off.
      Separated
      Someone who has wandered away from their friends. Most messages are written to a separated friend.
      In reach
      Two phones closer than the reach distance can pass messages. There is no internet and no mobile signal.
      Copy / carrier
      A phone holding a copy of a message "carries" it while its owner walks around, until it meets someone to pass it to.
      Copy allowance
      In the limited-copy methods the sender may create only a fixed number of copies. A carrier's number shows how many of those copies it controls; with 1 left it only gives the message to the friend.
      Delivered
      The message reached the friend's phone. The target is within 5 minutes; messages are deleted after 15 minutes.
      Hand-over (completed transfer)
      One copy moving from one phone to another. Fewer hand-overs means less phone activity (this is not a battery measurement).
      Within 5 min (D5)
      Share of all written messages that reached the friend within 5 minutes. Messages that never arrived still count, so they pull the share down.
      Within 15 min (D15)
      The same within 15 minutes, the message's whole lifetime.
      Delivery time (latency)
      How long a delivered message took. It ignores messages that never arrived, so read it together with D5 and D15.
      Spray phase
      In the limited-copy methods, the time while copies are still being handed out. It ends when every carrier holds its last copy and just waits to meet the friend.
