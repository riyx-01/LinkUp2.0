      What do these words mean?
      Person / phone
      Each dot is one festival-goer whose phone runs LinkUp. Other attendees are not shown.
      Walking / standing
      Filled dots are walking between areas; hollow dots are standing at a stage, the food court and so on.
      Friend group
      People who came together. They usually move together, but sometimes one wanders off.
      Separated
      Someone who has wandered away from their friends. Most messages are written to a separated friend.
      In reach
      Two phones closer than the reach distance can pass messages. There is no internet and no mobile signal.
      Copy / carrier
      A phone holding a copy of a message "carries" it while its owner walks around, until it meets someone to pass it to.
      Copy allowance
      In the limited-copy methods the sender may create only a fixed number of copies. A carrier's number shows how many of those copies it controls; with 1 left it only gives the message to the friend.
      Delivered
      The message reached the friend's phone. The target is within 5 minutes; messages are deleted after 15 minutes.
      Hand-over
      One copy moving from one phone to another. Fewer hand-overs means less phone activity (this is not a battery measurement).
